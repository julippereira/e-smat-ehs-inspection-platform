# Power BI Dashboard Pages

## Overview

The E-SMAT Power BI report contains six visible analytical pages and two hidden drill-through pages. The public version preserves the structure and navigation of the cloned report while replacing production information with synthetic inspection, questionnaire, employee, and operational data.

This document describes the purpose, principal visuals, filters, calculations, and navigation behavior of each page.

The screenshots referenced below are stored in:

```text
power-bi/screenshots/
```

Detailed semantic-model and DAX documentation is available in:

- [`data-model.md`](data-model.md)
- [`measures.md`](measures.md)

# Shared Report Structure

## Navigation

The visible pages use a shared navigation menu:

```text
Visão Geral
Registros
Atos Observados
Pessoas
Cargo
Consultar
```

The two drill-through pages remain hidden from the standard report navigation:

```text
DT_Registro
DT_Matricula
```

Users reach the hidden pages from a selected record or employee registration rather than through the main menu.

---

# 1. Visão Geral (Overview)

![Overview](./power-bi/screenshots/01-overview.png)

## Purpose

The Visão Geral page provides an executive summary of inspection activity and observed acts. It is the initial page of the report and combines methodology KPIs, act totals, thematic breakdowns, site comparisons, and monthly trends.

## KPI Cards

The page displays:

```text
EMAT                         24
ESMAT                        16
SMAT                         32
Atos Seguros              1,481
Atos Inseguros              341
Atos Inseguros por Registro 4.74
```

These totals reconcile with the synthetic model:

```text
72 inspection records
1,822 total observed acts
```

## Visuals

### Atos Observados por Tema e Classificação

Compares safe and unsafe acts across categories such as:

```text
Equipamentos de Proteção
Comportamento Seguro
Movimentação e Acesso
Organização do Trabalho
Resíduos
Máquinas e Ferramentas
Uso de Recursos
Emissões e Vazamentos
```

The visual uses the safe/unsafe classification and questionnaire category context.

### Atos Inseguros por Planta e Tema

Shows unsafe-act quantities by:

```text
Planta
Tema
```

The themes are:

```text
Meio Ambiente
Segurança
```

### Atos Seguros por Planta e Tema

Shows the same site and theme breakdown for safe acts.

### Evolução Mensal por Classificação

Displays safe and unsafe acts over the `periodo` field from `d_calendario`, using labels such as:

```text

mar-2026

```

# 2. Registros (Records)

![Records](./power-bi/screenshots/02-records.png)

## Purpose

The Registros page analyzes inspection volume rather than act quantities. It helps users identify where and when inspections were recorded across the operational hierarchy.

## Visuals

Compares distinct inspection records by workstation and methodology.

Blank workstation values remain valid because `Posto` is optional in the mock header data.


## Main Tables

```text

```

Unlike the Registros page, the values represent act quantities rather than inspection counts.

## Visuals

### Atos Observados por Segmento

Displays safe and unsafe acts by segment.

### Atos Observados por Planta

```text
SITE_D  457
SITE_A  447
SITE_C  345
SITE_B  248
SITE_E  188
SITE_F  137
```

These values sum to:


### Atos Observados por Posto

Shows act quantities by workstation. Records without a workstation can appear under a blank category.

# 4. Pessoas (People)

![People](./power-bi/screenshots/04-people.png)

## Purpose

The Pessoas page analyzes participation and observed acts by observer and observed-person registration.

The page combines inspection counts, average acts per observed person, act totals by observer, registration filters, and access to record-level detail.

## Page-Specific Filters

```text
Matrícula do Observador
Matrícula do Observado
Criador
```

## Visuals

### Registros por Observador

Shows distinct records by:

```text
Matrícula do Observador
Tipo de Registro
```

The public observer registrations use synthetic `900xxx` values.

### Média de Atos por Observado

Shows the ratio between observed acts and the number of valid observed-person registrations in each record.

The calculation uses:

```text
Total observed acts
÷
SUM(d_cabecalho[NumeroObservados])
```

`NumeroObservados` counts valid values in:

```text
MatriculaObservado
MatriculaObservado2
MatriculaObservado3
```

### Atos por Observador e Classificação

Displays safe and unsafe act quantities by observer registration.

### Detalhar Registro

The page contains a detail action for navigating from a selected record to the hidden `DT_Registro` page.

Depending on the selected visual and configured action, employee-level navigation can also direct the user to `DT_Matricula`.

## Main Tables and Measures

```text
d_cabecalho
d_classificacao
d_lista_matriculas
```

```text
[Registros por Matrícula]
[Atos Observados por Matrícula]
```

# 7. DT_Registro (Drill-Through Record)

![Drill-Through Record](./power-bi/screenshots/07-drill-through-record.png)

## Visibility

```text
Hidden drill-through page
```

## Purpose

`DT_Registro` displays the complete context and question-level results of one selected inspection.

The page is normally reached from the Consultar or Pessoas page after selecting an inspection.


# Mock Compatibility Notes

The public dashboard preserves the cloned layout and calculation behavior while using synthetic data.

The following differences are intentional:

- Employee names and registrations are synthetic;
- Plant and operational hierarchy values use neutral identifiers;
- Questionnaire descriptions are synthetic;
- Corporate email addresses are replaced with example addresses;
- The mock contains more segment values than the original filter layout was designed to show;
- Segment labels may appear truncated in screenshots;
- Optional workstations may appear blank;
- Hidden drill-through pages remain outside the standard navigation;
- Production source infrastructure is not included.

These differences do not change the documented analytical behavior of the report.
